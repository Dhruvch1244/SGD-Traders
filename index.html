Tutorial Briefing

Welcome to Hot-Cold!

An explosive physical party game where teams race against time, guided only by the voices of their friends calling out "Hot" or "Cold" to find hidden items or complete tasks.

Assemble & Split

Input your group. The game automatically divides everyone into 8 perfectly balanced teams. Turns rotate, and each team receives a randomly generated physical challenge.

Hiding & Preparing

When a turn starts, the team's active player covers their eyes or steps out. The rest of the players view the secret physical task and prepare the setup in the room.

Hot or Cold?

The timer fires! As the active player searches, the room physically shouts "Hotter!" (getting closer) or "Colder!" (getting further away). Complete the task within the 7-minute limit.

The Victory Lap

The team that completes their physical task with the fastest time dominates the leaderboard and takes home the ultimate title of Hot-Cold Champions!

Hot-Cold Rules Guide

Quick reference guide for the party championship.

1

Form 8 Teams

Players are shuffled and divided into 8 competing groups. Turns rotate sequence by sequence.

2

Hide and Setup

The active player covers their eyes. The team reveals the secret task and prepares the physical room setup.

3

Hot / Cold Shouting

The search starts. The room shouts "Hotter!" as they get close, and "Colder!" as they drift away from the target object.

4

Race the Timer

Complete the physical task before the 7-minute limit. The fastest team wins the trophy!
